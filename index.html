Active Muslims London

السلام عليكم / Greetings!

This group is for people who want to get active, love to be active, love the outdoors & love nature. Whether through hiking, biking, walking, kayaking, or simply volunteering, sharing and caring. A group to resonate with like-minded people, to enjoy activities together, share positive and enlightening experiences, make meaningful connections and create great memories.

Making such activities consistent and a habit fosters your sense of overall well-being and enables you to feel resilient and powerful. This group is open to all, including non-Muslims — we simply ask that you are considerate and respect the traditions, values and culture of the group and each other.

“Your body has a right over you” — regular activities are an investment in your mind, body, and soul.

🎦 Intro & Why It Matters

“It is often the case, that one is likely to have a more meaningful conversation whilst walking beside someone, looking forward, than facing each other in a coffee shop trying to make small talk.”

Our aim is to focus on getting more people active (particularly from within the Muslim communities), through outdoor activities such as walking, hiking and other fun events that usually involve movement. We try to share events with many facets — from facilitating quality connections by limiting numbers, to incorporating as many elements and points of interest as we can.

Why We Limit Numbers

We genuinely wish to provide a pleasant experience to each individual that attends our events, and we believe that smaller groups achieve this better. In our experience, a group under 30 works best for walking and hiking.

A smaller group allows participants enough time to properly talk to each other (if they wish), often resulting in stronger connections for any social get‑together afterwards. However, our focus remains the main activity — so it does not become just another mingling event where the activity becomes the excuse.

There are often first timers or newcomers who have had to build up the confidence to attend. Larger groups can be overwhelming — we wish to make it easier, comfortable and welcoming for them.

We limit numbers but repeat events frequently, allowing more opportunities to attend the same activity or meet different people.

It can also be for safety and consideration to other pedestrians, especially on narrow paths or busy areas. Larger groups tend to be slower, spread out further, and take longer for breaks — disrupting the flow.

RSVPs Matter

No-shows or late removals can hinder others from attending. Those who consistently RSVP but fail to show may prevent more reliable attendees from participating. If an event is full with a waitlist, we try to give priority to new and reliable attendees.

We are trying to do our tiny bit to encourage everyone to be more mindful, considerate, consistent and reliable towards others.

“Time is always beyond its value. Your time is your life. That is why the greatest gift you can give someone is your time.”
